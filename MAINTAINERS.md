## Wanted

AgentTaint has one maintainer, and wants more, especially from organizations
not already represented. Areas where help would make the biggest difference:

- fuzzy-match propagation and false-positive measurement,
- the planner/executor mode,
- AgentDojo-style benchmarks,
- the shared sink policy with AgentLock.

If you're interested, start with an issue labelled `good first issue` or
`help wanted`, or open an issue to say what you'd like to work on.
